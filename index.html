        05 · AI · the accelerating layer

        AI can copy the model quickly. It cannot inherit the evidence behind an approval.

        05.1The moat is the record, not the model

        In an open market, AI compresses the technical lead. It cannot copy the evidence behind an approved product: clinical validation, regulatory history and real-world performance accumulated over time. A newcomer may match the leader's scan-reading accuracy and still lack the record on which regulators, clinicians and buyers rely. The model can be rebuilt. The evidence must be earned again.

        05.2A rising bar can reverse the advantage

        The record usually protects the incumbent. That can change when the standard changes. Existing evidence may support the previous requirement, while a company designed for the new one is already collecting the data it demands. hmm looks for that transition: the point at which regulation changes what must be proven and a younger company is already proving it.

        08 · How we find them

        Where the record comes from.

        The one question

        If the necessities are where the next wave of innovation sits, and these three markets set the standards first, how do we find these founders?

        They form in markets the global funds treat as insular and thin on exits. The record beside this shows the innovation was there, mostly found or backed late.

        A

        Source before the first round

        These companies do not reliably enter through conventional venture networks. Many founders are still focused on the technical problem when hmm first approaches them. The fund maps a market, identifies relevant companies and contacts the teams before a formal raise begins. The GP used this outbound method in venture and later as an operator at a platform built to identify companies before they entered a sales pipeline.

        B

        Build the commercial side

        Technical capability is often already present. The unresolved questions are commercial: who buys, what budget pays, how large the reachable market is and how the company reaches its first customers. hmm works through those questions with the founders. The GP has spent a decade building and leading revenue functions for software and data companies selling across international markets.

        C

        Enter locally, scale internationally

        Most comparable exits in hmm's record are sales to overseas acquirers, while limited local capital continues to shape entry pricing. hmm invests before international capital becomes the price setter and helps founders build for the markets where their customers and eventual acquirers operate. The GP has worked on both sides of that expansion, leading commercial growth across APAC and into North America and Europe.
