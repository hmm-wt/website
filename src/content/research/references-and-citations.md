The fund's public research is citable. This page documents recommended citation formats per artefact, the primary-source datasets behind each headline figure, and the regulator portals the regulatory map draws from.

## How to cite

### The Necessity Index

> hmm Ventures, *The Necessity Index*, hmm.ventures/data/necessity-index/. APAC technology companies classified against the T1 / T2 framework across Australia, Japan, New Zealand, and Singapore.

### The Crystallisation Window calendar

> hmm Ventures, *The Crystallisation Window*, hmm.ventures/data/calendar/. Thirty-four in-flight regulations across the four target markets, 2024 to 2030.

### The Scoreboard

> hmm Ventures, *The Scoreboard*, hmm.ventures/data/scoreboard/. Five falsifiable predictions, dated and sourced.

### Research entries

> hmm Ventures, *[Entry title]*, hmm.ventures/research/[slug]/.

## Primary-source datasets

**Classified company universe.** 22,217 APAC technology companies; 19,416 in the four target markets. Enriched from licensed commercial data feeds. Classified against the T1 / T2 framework via a two-stage pipeline. Source file: `reference_dataset_classified.csv`.

**Regulations dataset.** 725 regulations across 64 countries and 13 sectors. Sourced from public legislation portals, regulator publications, and parliamentary records. Source file: `regulations_comprehensive.json`.

**Four-market sector-tier breakdown.** 174 four-market regulations reconciled by sector and tier. Source file: `four_market_sector_tier_breakdown.csv`.

## Regulator portal references

The regulatory map ingests publications from the following primary sources:

- **Australia.** ASIC, IP Australia, TGA, APRA, AEMO, FSANZ, ACCC.
- **Japan.** METI, MLIT, PMDA, MHLW, FSA, e-Gov.
- **New Zealand.** Medsafe, Callaghan Innovation, NZBN, ERA, MfE.
- **Singapore.** MAS, ACRA, HSA, LTA, EMA, MOH, IMDA.

Each row in the regulations dataset carries a primary-source URL to a regulator publication, an official gazette entry, or a parliamentary record. URLs are timestamped to publication date.

## Doctrine references

The hmm Necessity Doctrine is the long-form public companion to the Necessities. It is structured in ten chapters, with full citation footnotes published in the site-wide [References](/references/) page.

## Falsification commitments

The Scoreboard at /data/scoreboard/ publishes five falsifiable claims, each dated, sourced, and bound to a public resolution criterion. Charity-bound bets are accepted within the open betting window. The fund publishes resolution outcomes as each claim closes.
