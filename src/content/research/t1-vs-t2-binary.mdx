The framework is deliberately binary. Anything more elaborate collapses under the weight of subjective scoring.

## Tier 1. Licence-as-barrier.

A regulator has created or tightened a licensing regime, and the operational layer needed to comply with it is missing or under-built.

Examples: a digital health software classification under TGA, a new MAS payments licence category, an APRA prudential rule that demands fresh reporting infrastructure, an MLIT Level 4 autonomous-vehicle approval.

The barrier is *permission to operate*.

## Tier 2. Compulsion-as-barrier.

A regulator has not created a licence, but has compelled an industry to do something it was not previously required to do, and the compliance layer is missing.

Examples: mandatory embodied-carbon disclosure on construction projects, mandatory AI risk assessments under guardrails, mandatory critical-minerals provenance under dual-use rules.

The barrier is *evidence of compliance*.

## The split

Of the 725 regulations on the current map, **605 are Tier 1 and 120 are Tier 2**. The Tier 1 weighting is a feature, not a bug: licence regimes create harder, longer-lived barriers than compliance regimes, and the fund's preference order follows the same hierarchy.

## What the binary excludes

The framework deliberately excludes things that *look* regulated but are not, in practice, barriers a seed company can build a moat behind. Examples of out-of-scope:

- **Voluntary frameworks and ethics codes.** No statutory penalty, no licence dependence. No barrier.
- **Regulated buyers in non-mandated workflows.** A hospital is a regulated buyer, but if the workflow being sold into is not under statutory compliance, the regulatory layer is irrelevant to demand.
- **Disclosure rules with no enforcement.** Mandatory disclosure with no published enforcement record is not a T2 barrier.

The screen does not pretend that all regulation matters equally. The binary forces a clean call: licence or compulsion. If neither, the company is excluded regardless of regulatory-adjacent language in its marketing.

## Three design limits

The screen identifies opportunity, not winners. Founder quality determines who fills a category.

Monthly refresh cost on the map is budgeted for.

Jurisdictionally legible markets only. That is one reason the fund does not invest outside the four targets.
