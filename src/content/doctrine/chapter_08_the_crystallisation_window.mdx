The window is 2024–2028. Not a five-year planning convention. The overlap of specific timetables in the regulatory record.

> **Why 2024–2028**
>
> - **DORA enforceable:** 17 January 2025 (EU)[^1]
> - **EU AI Act:** prohibitions February 2025; GPAI obligations August 2025; high-risk obligations August 2026; full applicability August 2027[^2]
> - **FDA Final Rule 204:** enforceable 20 January 2026 (US)
> - **MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines:** transition through ~2027[^3]
> - **Japan AI Promotion Act:** in force June 2025; Strategy Headquarters operational September 2025[^4]
> - **EU CBAM:** definitive period from 1 January 2026

By 2028, every Necessity-aligned regulatory perimeter in each target market will have moved from drafting into enforcement.

---

## What crystallisation means

A regulatory regime crystallises when its enforcement architecture, its compliance expectations, and its market participants' adapted behaviour align. Before crystallisation, the regime is text. After crystallisation, it is the operating environment.

Companies that build during crystallisation inherit residual ambiguity (which is why they need regulatory familiarity) but also the procurement preferences and regulator relationships that compound after the regime settles. Companies that build after face a stable but more competitive market.

## The structural-advantage mechanism

A T1 company that holds a licence by 2026 has different optionality from one applying in 2029.<span class="pause" aria-label="pause"></span> Three differences are material.

**Regulator familiarity.** A T1 entity that has cleared an inspection cycle has a working relationship with the regulator. The next licence application, the next jurisdiction expansion, the next product variant all face lower friction.

**Procurement reference.** A T2 vendor deployed inside a regulated buyer during the build phase becomes the reference cited in submissions, audits, peer-bank conversations. By the time the regime settles, the vendor is the assumed default.

**Talent gravity.** Senior compliance and regulatory affairs talent moves toward firms that did the work first. Once the regime settles, the talent market re-prices.

---

## Where falsification sits

The window framing is wrong if one of three observable conditions appears.

**Regulatory rollback.** A major statutory regime repealed or materially weakened (EU AI Act stripped, FDA Rule 204 suspended, MAS guidelines withdrawn).

**Compliance commoditisation.** If compliance work becomes cheaper than the regulatory advantage it produces. AI compresses the compliance task but does not compress the licensing pathway.

**Exit market freeze.** If strategic acquirers or IPO markets close to regulated venture-backed companies, the thesis fails.

None of the three is currently active. Each is monitored.

---

## The two branching paths

By 2028, the four markets reach one of two outcomes. Either the regulatory architecture deepens and converges on common standards (path A). Or the architectures diverge under fiscal or political pressure (path B).

Both produce returns, through different mechanisms. Path A: portfolio compounding as procurement budgets grow. Path B: specialisation as funds adapt to multi-regime friction.
