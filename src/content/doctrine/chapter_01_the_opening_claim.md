**The Necessity Doctrine, Chapter 1**

---

Necessity is the thesis. Where regulation defines what an operator can legally launch, sell, export, insure, or continue running, the company that builds the compliant operational layer earns a structural premium. Four developed-APAC markets have built the deepest version of this architecture. That is where seed-stage value will be captured this decade.

The claim is falsifiable. The evidence is timestamped.

> **The four-market universe**
>
> - **19,416** classified companies across AU, JP, NZ, SG.[^1]
> - **33.5%** pass the conservative T1 / T2 screen. 6,505 companies in scope.[^1]
> - **2,625** are seed-stage in scope.[^1]
> - **174** in-flight regulations in the four markets. 117 T1 in the Five Necessities.[^2]
> - **34** in-flight regulations dated 2024 to 2030.[^2]

---

## What necessity means

A Necessity is a domain where society has decided market forces alone are insufficient. Food contamination. Missed diagnoses. Grid failure. Autonomous-system error. Compromised supply chains. Each is governed because demand in it cannot be allowed to fail.

Regulation in these domains is not friction. It is the floor of operation. Capital that flows into building the proof layer flows into the most defensible category of work available at seed.

---

## Where the capital is going

In the four markets, ~USD 7 billion was deployed in 2024.[^3] Capital absorbed at Series C runs 82% to regulated companies. Cumulative funding multiples widen from 1.09x at pre-seed to 2.12x at Series D+.[^3]

The regulatory build is in flight. DORA enforceable from 17 January 2025. EU AI Act phasing from February 2025. Japan's AI Promotion Act passed May 2025. Thirty-four in-flight regulations dated 2024 to 2030 across the four markets. The doctrine names this period the *Crystallisation Window*.

---

## The conservative classifier

One in three four-market companies passes the T1 / T2 screen. The seed-stage subset is the doctrine's addressable universe.[^1]

Tier 1 is licence-as-barrier: a company cannot legally operate without regulatory approval. Tier 2 is compulsion-as-barrier: the buyer is statutorily obligated to purchase compliant solutions. Chapter 3 walks the mechanism.

Barriers created by regulation compound. Barriers created by capital or network effects decay.

---

## Where the premium shows up

The empirical anchors are in the funding ladder.[^3]

- **Seed:** 1.12x. Near-parity.
- **Series A:** 1.19x median.
- **Series C:** 1.53x median. The differential opens.
- **Series D+:** 1.42x; cumulative funding multiple 2.12x.
- **Exit rate:** regulated 13.41% vs unregulated 6.03%. 2.22x.

Enter at near-parity. Exit at premium. The regulatory perimeter pre-clears the field. Surviving operators harvest a premium that compounds with every regulatory event their compliance layer satisfies.

---

## What this means at seed

In regulated technology, the markup from seed to Series A reflects the barrier crossed, not just revenue growth. The capital-efficient window is the period before approval. That window is where the doctrine underwrites.<span class="pause" aria-label="pause"></span>

Five domains meet the test: Food Security, Biomedical, Power, Autonomous, Critical Materials. Chapter 2 walks the empirical anchors behind each.
