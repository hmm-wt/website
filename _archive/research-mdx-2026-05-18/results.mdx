## Why the rate compounds

Three mechanisms.

Cleared milestones are sticky. A TGA SaMD clearance, MAS Digital Bank licence, or PMDA SAKIGAKE designation does not expire on the same cycle as a product release.

Cross-jurisdiction recognition matters. A T1 company with FDA 510(k) is materially closer to TGA, HSA, and Medsafe than a competitor starting from zero. The compliance trail is portable.

Acquirer demand is structural. Strategic acquirers cannot organically build the compliance layer at the pace the regulatory clock demands. Acquisition is the rational response.

## What this is not

This is not a claim that T1 / T2 is the only path to venture returns. Unregulated companies produce outsized exits. The claim is narrower: inside the four-market regulated-sector seed thesis, the stage-graduation curve is materially steeper than the unregulated comparable, and the exit dataset is verifiable per row.
